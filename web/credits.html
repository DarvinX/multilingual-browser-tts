Model credits

Models and training data

Tamal Hansda trained the compact voices for Bengali, Gujarati, Hindi, Malayalam, Telugu, and English. The acoustic model adapts Tiny Voca by Muhammed Nazeem under Apache 2.0. Training pipeline and model diagrams

Maya 2 Native (Apache 2.0) generated the training audio with its built-in voices and the Kyutai Mimi codec (CC BY 4.0). Each dataset card lists the voice, prompt sources, and license terms. Dataset and model cards

Pronunciation and Roman input

  • The Bengali phonemizer uses the Bengali pronunciation lexicon by Google Inc. (CC BY 4.0). Its English loanword fallback uses the CMU Pronouncing Dictionary (BSD-style license).
  • The Gujarati, Hindi, Malayalam, Telugu, and English phonemizers were trained with pronunciation labels from eSpeak NG.
  • The Gujarati, Hindi, Malayalam, and Telugu Roman-input converters adapt Dakshina training lexicons by Google Research (CC BY-SA 4.0).
