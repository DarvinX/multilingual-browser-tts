Model credits

The Bengali voice was trained on synthetic Bengali speech with the Tiny Voca architecture (Apache 2.0).

  • Maya 2 Native generated the Jahnavi training audio (Apache 2.0). Maya uses Kyutai Mimi for audio decoding (CC BY 4.0).
  • Training prompts came from Mozilla Common Voice's Bengali Sentence Collector (CC0 1.0).
  • English pronunciation fallback uses the CMU Pronouncing Dictionary (BSD-style license).
  • The Gujarati, Hindi, Kannada, Malayalam, Tamil and Telugu Roman-input converters adapt the Dakshina training lexicons by Google Research under CC BY-SA 4.0. Alternate spellings were ranked against language-specific prompt text.
  • The speech engine runs with ONNX Runtime Web (MIT).
  • The Bengali interface uses Noto Sans Bengali (SIL Open Font License).
